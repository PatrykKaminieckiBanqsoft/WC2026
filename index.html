ℹ️ About

WC2026 | Banqsoft Predictions — a team prediction game. State (players, picks, results) lives in a free online JSON store (JSONBin.io). One link, everyone plays — no logins, no installs.

How to play: predict the exact score for each of the 104 matches. After the group stage, admin fills in the bracket pairings (Round of 32, 16, etc.). Points are awarded once admin enters official results (admin password: Kajtek). Highest score wins.
